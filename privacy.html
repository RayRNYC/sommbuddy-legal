Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026  ·  DataGuy 5280 LLC

1. Information We Collect

SOMMBuddy collects the following information to operate the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you create an account via email, Google, or Apple Sign In.
  • Wine data — wine names, producers, vintages, your personal ratings, tasting notes, and label photos you choose to save to your cellar.
  • Camera and photos — label images you capture or select are sent to our AI service for text extraction. Images are not stored on our servers beyond the duration of the request.
  • Usage data — monthly scan and comparison counts used to enforce plan limits.
  • Device information — platform type (iOS/Android) for feature compatibility purposes.
  • Crash reports — anonymised error and crash information to help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the SOMMBuddy service, including AI-powered label scanning and sommelier descriptions.
  • To sync your cellar, ratings, and preferences across your devices.
  • To enforce plan limits and process subscription payments.
  • To diagnose crashes, bugs, and service errors.
  • To communicate important service changes to you.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following services only as necessary to operate SOMMBuddy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), and server-side Cloud Functions. Data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure (US region). Google Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — wine label images and text queries are sent to Anthropic's API for AI processing. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management. RevenueCat receives your App Store / Google Play purchase receipt to verify subscriptions. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Sentry — anonymised crash and error reporting to help us fix issues. Sentry Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is flagged for deletion and permanently removed within 90 days.

You can delete your account at any time via Account → Delete Account in the app.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at DataGuy5280@gmail.com. Account deletion can also be performed directly through the app.

6. Children

SOMMBuddy is a wine-related app intended for adults of legal drinking age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase Authentication, HTTPS encryption in transit, and Firestore security rules that restrict data access to the account owner. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Your continued use of SOMMBuddy after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at DataGuy5280@gmail.com.
