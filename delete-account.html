Delete your SOMMBuddy account

DataGuy 5280 LLC

Delete it in the app

  • Open SOMMBuddy and sign in.
  • Go to Account, scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete.
  • Confirm. If you signed in with Apple or Google, you may be asked to confirm with them.

No longer have the app?

Email DataGuy5280@gmail.com from the email address on your SOMMBuddy account, with the subject Delete my SOMMBuddy account. We'll confirm by reply and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile and preferences
  • Your cellar, wishlist, ratings, tasting notes and label photos
  • Your plan and usage records

Your sign-in account is removed at once. The rest is kept for 90 days and then permanently deleted, as described in our Privacy Policy. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and crash reports held by Sentry follow those services' own retention policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus or Cellar Pro subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or billing continues.
